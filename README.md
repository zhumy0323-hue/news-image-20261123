A front-page image summing up Monday 23 November 2026's world news in 35 stories across 6 continents and 17 fields: the EU proposes sweeping new Russia sanctions, Ukraine says its naval drones sank a Russian corvette off Libya, and Nvidia brings back Jensen Huang as CEO — plus a UTC timeline of the day, a verification badge on every drawn story, a world map, and a dated list of 22 more stories.

Every story happened or was first reported on Monday 23 November 2026. Sunday events appear only as Monday developments: the Benghazi strikes claimed by Zelenskyy on Monday, the Cikande factory-fire search resuming, and the 49ers' Mexico City win reported Monday. The image's DATE CHECK box names what was deliberately left out as earlier news: the COP31 collapse and the Dymer first toll (Sun 22 Nov), Boca's Copa Sudamericana win and the El Chapo Isidro capture (Sat 21 Nov), the Peru quake and the Nvidia SEC probe (Fri 20 Nov), and the World Court's Rohingya ruling (Thu 19 Nov).

## Run it
